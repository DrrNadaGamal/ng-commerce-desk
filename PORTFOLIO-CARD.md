## NG Commerce Desk

Custom WordPress / WooCommerce plugin, working synthetic-data portfolio demo.

Effect: an original operations layer that brings demo sales trends, recent orders,
stock flags and guarded edits into a focused interface. WooCommerce's existing
commerce tools remain available; this is not a claim they were absent.

Premium details: English/Arabic RTL, mobile table-to-card layouts, restrained
indigo accents, local Inter/IBM Plex fonts, date/search/status filters, empty/error
states, role restrictions, stale-write checks and CSV exports.

Before/After caption: Controlled demonstration using the same synthetic WooCommerce
data. Baseline is an intentionally built plain-list fixture, not standard WooCommerce
or a real client website. No speed, conversion or revenue uplift measured.

AI-assisted implementation under Nada Gamal's direction. Demo only, no real shop
connection, payments or outgoing emails. FR/DE locales are machine-assisted and
not professionally reviewed. Tested only on the stack listed in TEST-REPORT.md.
